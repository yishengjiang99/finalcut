FinalCap Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-26 · Last updated: 2026-10-10 · Product: FinalCap (iOS + web) at grepawk.com · Operator: grepawk / Yisheng Jiang

This policy describes how FinalCap handles information when you use our iOS app and website. It does not cover unrelated grepawk products (for example Grok Camera / Photo Recipes) unless you use those separately.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email and provider identifiers if you sign in (Apple, Google, or other methods we enable).
  • Photos and videos (iOS) — on iPhone, your photos and videos stay on your device. Edits are applied and exported on the iPhone. We do not upload your media unless you turn on Settings → Cloud processing (off by default); with it on, a clip is uploaded only for an edit the iPhone can't do yet, such as server captions.
  • Edit requests — your typed or dictated request, basic media details (type, size, duration, whether there is audio), and up to 4 small still frames of your edit, sent so the AI understands your clip. On the web, your video, photo or audio is edited in your browser (FFmpeg running on your device) and is not uploaded. If a step cannot run in your browser, the app asks first and uploads only when you explicitly agree.
  • Captions (on-device, default) — when you ask for captions on the web, speech is transcribed in your browser and nothing is uploaded. The speech model is served from our own servers (same place as the app) and cached in your browser after the first use.
  • Cloud captions (optional, off by default) — if you turn this on, only the extracted audio track (never the video) is uploaded to our server and sent to OpenAI for transcription; our server deletes the uploaded audio right after transcription.
  • Lyric captions (optional) — runs only when you ask for lyric captions. Your video's audio is uploaded to our server and sent to OpenAI (whisper-1 model) to transcribe it; our server deletes the uploaded audio right after transcription. The transcript text (not the audio) is then sent to xAI (Grok, which may search the web) to identify the song, look up its lyrics, and translate the lines. Your audio is never sent to xAI. On the web, the captions are burned into your video in your browser, the same as other web edits (on iOS and on older web flows they were burned on our server). On iOS, lyric captions runs only with Cloud processing on, after asking you before uploading, and follows the same OpenAI/xAI handling.
  • Microphone and speech (iOS) — used only for dictation. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone and the audio is never uploaded. Only the resulting text is sent as your edit request.
  • Purchases — App Store transaction identifiers (iOS) or Stripe customer/subscription ids (web). We do not receive your full card number from Apple.
  • Diagnostics — optional crash or performance logs if enabled.
  • Usage analytics (web) — anonymous product events recorded on our own servers so we can see which parts of the site work: page views of the landing page, which editing tool ran and whether it succeeded, that a file was opened (type and a coarse size range, not the name), exports, sign-in and upgrade steps, and the referring website's domain and campaign tags. To count repeat visits, the site stores a random ID in your browser's local storage, plus a per-tab session ID. We use no cookies and no third-party analytics for this, we do not store your IP address, and we never record your prompts, file names, media, frames, or account details. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, no events are sent.

How we use information

  • Provide editing, captioning (including lyric captions and translation), preview, and export.
  • Authenticate you and enforce quotas or subscriptions.
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse.
  • Comply with law and App Store / payment provider rules.

Sharing

We use subprocessors for hosting, storage, speech-to-text, model inference, and payments (for example cloud hosts; OpenAI for speech-to-text and AI models; xAI for Grok AI models, including lyric lookup and translation; Apple; and Stripe on web). The on-device speech model is served from our own servers. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

Uploaded media and job outputs are kept as needed to provide the service and then deleted or anonymized according to our retention schedule and your deletion requests, subject to legal holds. For lyric captions, the uploaded audio is deleted right after transcription, and the transcript lines and translations are kept in server memory for up to 30 minutes so the caption burn-in step can use them, then expire.

Anonymous usage analytics events are deleted automatically after 13 months.

Your choices

  • You can request access or deletion by emailing the support address below.
  • You can revoke Sign in with Apple/Google in system settings.
  • You can manage or cancel iOS subscriptions in Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • You can reset the anonymous analytics ID by clearing this site's data in your browser, or turn analytics off by enabling Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Children

FinalCap is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.
