FinalCap Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-26 · Last updated: 2026-10-18 · Product: FinalCap (iOS + web) at grepawk.com · Operator: grepawk / Yisheng Jiang

This policy describes how FinalCap handles information when you use our iOS app and website. It does not cover unrelated grepawk products (for example Grok Camera / Photo Recipes) unless you use those separately.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email and provider identifiers if you sign in (Apple, Google, or other methods we enable).
  • Photos and videos (iOS) — on iPhone, your photos and videos stay on your device. Edits are applied and exported on the iPhone. We do not upload your media unless you turn on Settings → Cloud processing (off by default); with it on, a clip is uploaded only for an edit the iPhone can't do yet, such as server captions.
  • Edit requests — your typed or dictated request, basic media details (type, size, duration, whether there is audio), and up to 4 small still frames of your edit, sent so the AI understands your clip. On the web, your video, photo or audio is edited in your browser (FFmpeg running on your device) and is not uploaded. If a step cannot run in your browser, the app asks first and uploads only when you explicitly agree.
  • Captions (on-device, default) — when you ask for captions on the web, speech is transcribed in your browser and nothing is uploaded. The speech model is served from our own servers (same place as the app) and cached in your browser after the first use.
  • Cloud captions (optional, off by default) — if you turn this on, only the extracted audio track (never the video) is uploaded to our server and sent to OpenAI for transcription; our server deletes the uploaded audio right after transcription.
  • Lyric captions (optional) — runs only when you ask for lyric captions. Your video's audio is uploaded to our server and sent to OpenAI (whisper-1 model) to transcribe it; our server deletes the uploaded audio right after transcription. The transcript text (not the audio) is then sent to xAI (Grok, which may search the web) to identify the song, look up its lyrics, and translate the lines. Your audio is never sent to xAI. On the web, the captions are burned into your video in your browser, the same as other web edits (on iOS and on older web flows they were burned on our server). On iOS, lyric captions runs only with Cloud processing on, after asking you before uploading, and follows the same OpenAI/xAI handling.
  • Microphone and speech (iOS) — used only for dictation. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone and the audio is never uploaded. Only the resulting text is sent as your edit request.
  • Notifications (iOS) — if you allow notifications, the app sends our server the device's push token, your time zone and app version, linked to the app's random device ID (not your name or email). While notifications are allowed, the app also reports a few event names with a time: that the app was opened, that an export finished, that the Pro screen was shown, and that you tapped a notification. Together with the number of edits per day we already count for quotas, these decide which tip to send and when (for example, a tip about captions a few days after install). We never use your prompts, captions or media for this. Tips are limited to at most one a day and three a week, are not sent at night in your time zone, and promotional messages ("Tips & offers") are sent only if you turn that on in Settings. To word a notification, our server sends xAI (Grok) only a short anonymous summary, such as "free plan, 1–3 edits last week, has exported", with no identifiers.
  • Purchases — App Store transaction identifiers (iOS) or Stripe customer/subscription ids (web). We do not receive your full card number from Apple.
  • Diagnostics — optional crash or performance logs if enabled.
  • Usage analytics (web) — anonymous product events recorded on our own servers so we can see which parts of the site work: page views of the landing page, which editing tool ran and whether it succeeded, that a file was opened (type and a coarse size range, not the name), exports, sign-in and upgrade steps, and the referring website's domain and campaign tags. To count repeat visits, the site stores a random ID in your browser's local storage, plus a per-tab session ID. We use no cookies and no third-party analytics for this, we do not store your IP address, and we never record your prompts, file names, media, frames, or account details. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, no events are sent.

Streaming captions (optional)

When you ask FinalCap on the web to caption speech in another language (for example "add captions in Chinese"), this happens only if you tap Send audio. We ask every time. If you tap Not now, no audio leaves your browser.

  • What leaves your browser. Your browser takes the audio from your video and streams only the audio, never the picture, directly to ElevenLabs (Scribe v2 Realtime) to transcribe it. It connects with a short-lived, single-use token that our server issues. The audio does not pass through our servers, and we keep no copy of it.
  • What ElevenLabs may keep. ElevenLabs may keep the audio and the transcript under its own privacy policy (elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy). We cannot delete them from ElevenLabs, and we have not enabled any zero-retention mode. This is the one case where we cannot delete your audio.
  • What we receive. Your browser sends us the transcript text (not the audio). Our server forwards that text to xAI (Grok) to translate it and returns the result. Our server holds the text in memory only while it handles the request and does not store it.
  • The video. The captions are burned into your video in your browser. If your browser cannot do that, the app asks you before it uploads the video to our server for that one step.

How we use information

  • Provide editing, captioning (including lyric captions and translation), preview, and export.
  • Authenticate you and enforce quotas or subscriptions.
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse.
  • Comply with law and App Store / payment provider rules.

Sharing

We use subprocessors for hosting, storage, speech-to-text, model inference, and payments (for example cloud hosts; OpenAI for speech-to-text and AI models; ElevenLabs for speech-to-text on streaming captions (your browser sends the audio to it directly, and it keeps data under its own policy); xAI for Grok AI models, including lyric lookup, translation, and wording notifications from an anonymous summary; Apple, including Apple Push Notification service to deliver notifications; and Stripe on web). The on-device speech model is served from our own servers. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

Uploaded media and job outputs are kept as needed to provide the service and then deleted or anonymized according to our retention schedule and your deletion requests, subject to legal holds. For lyric captions, the uploaded audio is deleted right after transcription, and the transcript lines and translations are kept in server memory for up to 30 minutes so the caption burn-in step can use them, then expire. For streaming captions the audio goes directly from your browser to ElevenLabs, which keeps the audio and transcript under its own policy and which we cannot ask to delete; we hold no copy, and the transcript text is not stored on our server.

Anonymous usage analytics events are deleted automatically after 13 months. Notification events and the log of notifications sent are deleted after 13 months; a push token is disabled as soon as Apple reports it is no longer valid (for example after the app is deleted).

Your choices

  • You can request access or deletion by emailing the support address below.
  • You can revoke Sign in with Apple/Google in system settings.
  • You can manage or cancel iOS subscriptions in Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • You can turn off promotional notifications in FinalCap → Settings → Tips & offers, and all notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → FinalCap. Turning notifications off stops the notification events described above.
  • You can reset the anonymous analytics ID by clearing this site's data in your browser, or turn analytics off by enabling Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Children

FinalCap is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.
