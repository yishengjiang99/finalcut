AI Camera - Music Reader Support

Need help reading or playing sheet music with the app?

Contact

Email yisheng.jiang@gmail.com with:

  • iOS version and device model
  • What you scanned (a photo of the page helps)
  • Whether the problem was the camera, recognition, playback, or the library

Tips

  • Scan in good light, with the page flat and filling the frame.
  • Printed sheet music works best. Handwritten music may not be recognized.
