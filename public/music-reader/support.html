AI Camera - Music Reader Support

AI Camera - Music Reader photographs printed sheet music, reads it on your device, and plays it back (SF2 / MIDI) with practice tools. Free tier: 5 page scans per day. Music Reader Pro unlocks unlimited scans.

Contact

Email yisheng.jiang@gmail.com with your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and, if you can, a photo of the page that did not read correctly. Say whether the problem was the camera, recognition, playback, library, quota, or purchases.

Tips for good results

  • Use clean, printed (engraved) sheet music. Handwritten music is not supported.
  • Photograph the page straight on, in even light, with the whole page in frame.
  • After a successful read, use tempo, instrument, A–B loop, and hand mute/solo in the player to practice.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel Music Reader Pro in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Restore purchases from the in-app paywall if you reinstall.
