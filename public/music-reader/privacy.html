AI Camera - Music Reader Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-06 · App: AI Camera - Music Reader (iOS and iPadOS) · Operator: GrepAwk LLC

This policy describes how AI Camera - Music Reader handles information. It does not cover other GrepAwk products (for example FinalCap or AI Camera - Auto Recipes).

Summary

Sheet-music recognition and playback run entirely on your device. Your photos and scores are never uploaded. The app has no account or sign-in. The app does send anonymous usage events to our servers to improve the product, and processes in-app subscriptions through Apple.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera, or a photo you choose from your library, only to read the sheet music in that image. The image is processed on your device by machine-learning models bundled with the app. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party. Scans, MIDI files, and titles the app creates are saved only on your device. Deleting a scan or the app removes them.

Anonymous usage analytics

The app posts anonymous event telemetry to https://photo.grepawk.com/api/telemetry (first-party). Events may include app open, scan start/success/fail, playback-related funnel steps, paywall views, and purchase start/success/fail. Each event includes an anonymous id and session id stored in on-device preferences, plus non-sensitive props such as app version or error type. We do not send photos, MIDI, audio, email, phone numbers, precise location, or account credentials.

Purchases

Music Reader Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. We store an on-device entitlement flag so Pro features (unlimited scans) work offline. We may record anonymous purchase-funnel events (product id, success/fail) in the telemetry above. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Accounts, advertising and tracking

There is no account or sign-in. The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Crash reports

If you opt in to sharing diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymized crash reports with us. We use them only to fix bugs.

TestFlight

When you test a beta build through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback and crash information you choose to send, as described in Apple’s TestFlight terms.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Anonymous telemetry may still fire on any device that runs the app.

Open source

The app is open source under the AGPL-3.0: github.com/yishengjiang99/omr-sheet-cam.
