AI Camera - Music Reader Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-26 · Product: AI Camera - Music Reader (iOS) · Operator: grepawk / Yisheng Jiang

This policy describes how AI Camera - Music Reader handles information. It does not cover other grepawk products (for example FinalCap or AI Camera - Auto Recipes).

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or receive any personal information.

  • Camera and photos — used only to read the sheet music you point the camera at or pick from your library. Recognition runs entirely on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded.
  • Scans and music — the MIDI files and titles the app creates are saved only on your device. Deleting a scan or the app removes them.
  • Purchases — the app has no in-app purchases.
  • Diagnostics — if you opt in to share crash data with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us anonymous crash reports.

Sharing

We do not share or sell any information, because we do not have any. If you choose to share a MIDI file using the iOS share sheet, it goes only where you send it.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Open source

The app is open source under the AGPL-3.0: github.com/yishengjiang99/omr-sheet-cam.
